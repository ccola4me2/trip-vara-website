Putting an agency on

The whole sequence, in order, with what goes wrong at each step.

Only the portal owner can do any of this. An agency owner runs their own book and their own advisors; creating an agency, moving somebody between agencies and making somebody an owner are all refused for them, on purpose. An owner who could promote their own staff could hand out sight of every book in their agency without anybody outside it knowing.

Budget twenty minutes, most of it waiting for the other person.

Before you start

One thing has to exist that is not in this portal: somebody at the agency who will be its owner. That is the person who approves their advisors, sets commission agreements and maintains the agency record. Have their email to hand and tell them the link is coming.

Nothing else needs setting up outside the portal. This used to start with creating a CRM account for the agency. There is no CRM now and the portal holds everything.

The sequence

1

Create the agency

Agencies → Add an agency. The name is what their clients see on a quote, a statement and a deal page, so it is the trading name rather than anything internal.

Fill in the address, phone and seller-of-travel registration while you are there. They appear on everything the agency's clients read, and the registration number is there because in several states it has to be.

Give the agency its own automation partition on the same form. It is a leftover name from the old CRM and it is still the key that decides which advisors an automation or a form reaches. Every advisor who signs up inherits their agency's value as they are created, so an agency left blank puts its advisors in one partition with every other agency that has none. Anything unique will do; the agency's own slug is a reasonable choice. Setting it later does not move advisors already created, so it is worth doing now.

2

Turn on signups and send the link

Tick open for signups and save. The agency's row then shows a link of the form /join/<slug> with a Copy link button. That page is branded as the agency and is the only way in.

With signups off, anybody following the link is told plainly that the agency is not taking signups rather than being filed as pending and left waiting. Turn it off again once their advisors are on, so the link stops accepting strangers.

3

They sign up

They choose their own password. Nobody, here or there, ever sees it or sets it for them. The account arrives pending and cannot sign in.

If they say the link did not work, the usual cause is signups being switched off again, or the wrong agency's link.

4

Approve them

Advisors, filter to Pending approval, press Approve. They can sign in from that moment.

5

Make them their agency's owner

On their row, set the agency to the one you created and the role to owner. Leave the portal owner flag off. That flag is the keys to the whole platform, not to their agency: it lets somebody create agencies, move advisors between them and promote other owners. An agency owner needs none of that.

Only the first person needs this. Everybody else at that agency signs up through the same link and is approved by them.

6

Hand over

Tell them the three things that are theirs from now on: approving their own advisors, setting each advisor's commission agreement, and keeping the agency record current. Point them at How this works, which covers the rest.

What goes wrong

  • "The join link says they are not taking signups." Signups are off on that agency, or the slug in the link is another agency's. Both are on the Agencies page.
  • One agency's automations are firing on another's clients. Both agencies were created without an automation partition, so their advisors share the default one. Fixing the agency record does not move advisors already created: set the agency first, then each advisor on Advisors.
  • They cannot approve their own advisors. Their role is still advisor rather than owner, or they are not attached to the agency. Step 5.
  • They can see agencies that are not theirs. The portal owner flag is on. Turn it off. Nobody outside this portal should ever have it.
  • Nothing is emailing anybody. That is portal-wide rather than per-agency: it needs RESEND_API_KEY on the Worker. The Advisors page says whether it is set.

Taking an agency off

There is no delete, deliberately. Suspend each of its advisors on Advisors, which ends their sessions immediately, and turn signups off so the join link stops accepting anybody. Their reservations, clients and commission history stay where they are, which is what you want if there is ever a question about what was sold.
