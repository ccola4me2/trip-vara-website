Paste one confirmation

Paste the confirmation email from the vendor and this reads what it can off it: the number, the ship, the dates, the cabin, the guests, the money. Nothing is saved until you have checked it, and every field shows the line it came from, because vendor confirmations have no standard shape and this will misread some of them.
